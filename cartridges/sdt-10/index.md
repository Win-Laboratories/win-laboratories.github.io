# SDT-10

*Placeholder: the SDT-10 semiconductor disc transducer.*

The earliest report logged so far is from February 1975: <em>High Fidelity</em> reportedly says the SDT-10 was "based directly on the Euphonics (whose design and patents Win has acquired) but has been re-engineered in a number of significant respects". This has not been checked yet. Until it is, don't describe the SDT-10 as either Win's original invention or a Euphonics copy.

The 1976 ETI lead mentions an unnamed "new Win pickup cartridge". It may or may not be the SDT-10.
