# Other designs

*Placeholder: other Win tonearms.*

The 1976 ETI lead mentions an unnamed "Win tonearm". Don't assume it is the SDA-10 or the pantograph arm. The Lab 10 turntable of 1974 came with a "low-mass tone arm … of hand-polished wood".
